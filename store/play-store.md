# Play Store Listing

## Title

XCaro - Gomoku Online

## Short Description

Play Gomoku online or vs AI. Ranked matches, chat, leaderboard. Free!

## Full Description

XCaro brings the classic Gomoku/Caro strategy board game to mobile with fast
online matches, offline AI practice, and a clean modern interface. Place stones
on a 15×15 board and be the first player to make five in a row horizontally,
vertically, or diagonally.

Create a room and invite a friend with a short room code, or join an existing
match and play in real time. WebSocket-powered gameplay keeps moves, turns, game
results, and chat synchronized between players.

Practice your tactics against multiple AI difficulties. Easy mode helps new
players learn the board, Medium provides balanced play, and Hard offers a deeper
challenge for experienced Gomoku fans.

Compete in ranked online games with Elo ratings and a global leaderboard. Track
your progress through match history, player profiles, stats, and recent results.

XCaro is designed for quick casual games and thoughtful strategic play: smooth
animations, dark/light themes, clear board visuals, sound toggles, and responsive
layouts for phones and tablets.

## Content Rating

Everyone. XCaro contains no violence, adult content, gambling, or user-generated
media beyond short in-game chat messages.
