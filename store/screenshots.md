# Store Screenshot Plan

Capture screenshots on both Android and iOS before release.

## Device Requirements

- Android: 6.7-inch phone layout, portrait orientation
- iOS: 6.5-inch iPhone layout, portrait orientation
- Use production-like data: real usernames, realistic leaderboard entries, and
  completed games without test/debug labels
- Hide development server URLs, tokens, and system debug overlays

## Required Scenes

1. **Home and Match Entry**
   - Caption: `Play online, join by room code, or practice vs AI.`
   - Show the home screen with create room, join room, AI, leaderboard, and
     history entry points.

2. **Online Match Board**
   - Caption: `Classic 15×15 Gomoku with real-time turns.`
   - Show an in-progress online match with several black/white stones and a
     visible turn indicator.

3. **In-Game Chat**
   - Caption: `Chat with your opponent during friendly matches.`
   - Show the game board with the chat panel open and safe example messages.

4. **AI Difficulty Selection**
   - Caption: `Practice against Easy, Medium, or Hard AI.`
   - Show the AI setup or AI game screen with difficulty controls visible.

5. **Leaderboard and Profile**
   - Caption: `Climb the Elo leaderboard and review your stats.`
   - Show the top players list or a public profile with rank, rating, win rate,
     and recent games.

## Optional Extra Scenes

- Email verification prompt
- Game history and stats
- Dark theme board
- Onboarding tutorial step
